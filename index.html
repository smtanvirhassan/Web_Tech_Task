The Story

Messi grew up in Rosario and joined Barcelona's youth academy at 13. He debuted for the first team in 2004 and became the club's defining player, known for close control, vision and a low centre of gravity. In December 2022 he lifted the FIFA World Cup with Argentina, the one major trophy that had eluded him. Many fans consider that the moment that settled the greatest-of-all-time debate.



Achievements

  • 8 Ballon d'Or awards
  • 10 La Liga titles
  • 4 UEFA Champions League titles
  • 1 FIFA World Cup title
  • 1 Copa America title
